# Repository instructions

- This is an independent clean-room learning repository for one bounded hotel-reservation invariant.
- Keep search visibility, quote, inventory hold, booking confirmation, payment authorization/capture, room assignment, check-in,
  stay completion, refund, and external acceptance as separate facts.
- Never add real credentials, guest/property/staff identities, contact or payment data, confirmation codes, production rates,
  internal topology, or copied third-party prose, diagrams, images, tables, fixtures, benchmarks, or code.
- Preserve unrelated work. Stage exact paths, keep commits single-purpose, and do not add co-author trailers unless requested.
- Infrastructure-dependent conclusions require the matching real service or true-process boundary in public CI; mocks cannot prove
  durable inventory conservation, lease expiry, idempotent booking, fencing, or recovery.
- A benchmark is a bounded synthetic observation for its exact fixture and runtime, never demand, revenue, occupancy, SLA, or
  production-capacity evidence.
